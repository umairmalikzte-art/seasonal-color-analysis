# Seasonal Color Analysis – keyword research and content structure

Research date: November 2026. Markets: US, UK, Canada, Australia.

**What this is based on.** Ahrefs and Semrush were not connected, so there
are no search volumes or keyword difficulty (KD) scores in this file. The
keywords come from the pages that rank on Google for the core queries, from
competitor URL slugs and headings, and from the PRD. Before launch, check
volume and KD in Google Keyword Planner, Ahrefs or Semrush and reorder the
priorities if needed. Do not add made-up numbers to this file.

## 1. Competitors reviewed

| Site | What ranks | Tools | Weak points we can beat |
|---|---|---|---|
| seasonapproved.com | 12 subtype palette pages with 36 hex codes each, about 10 "X vs Y" pages, makeup pages, "can I be between two seasons" | Shopping app, "is this color in my season", AI stylist (premium trial) | The free value is a funnel into the app. No free quiz on the pages read. It mixes two sets of season names. |
| theconceptwardrobe.com | 12 "comprehensive guide" pages (uses Dark Autumn and Dark Winter) | None | No tools, no hex codes, no comparisons. Paid wardrobe guide. |
| mystylebox.ca | 12 season guides, flow theory, body shape and style personality | Quiz, paid online analysis, paid palettes | Palettes and makeup guides are paid. Thin hex data. |
| elfcosmetics.com | "color analysis quiz" | Pinterest quiz | Mainly brand marketing, 4 seasons only |
| facetuneapp.com | "what is my color season quiz" | App photo analysis | Needs an app and a photo |
| sterlingstyleacademy.com | "12 seasonal color analysis", soft summer vs soft autumn | None (courses) | Written for students of the course, not end users |
| Media (AOL, Today, Grazia, Her Campus) | "what season am I", "how to find your colors" | None | First-person stories, no tools, no full palettes |
| boredpanda, designdash, tvovermind | "soft autumn color palette" | None | Many results are about **interior design**. This is a different intent that we do not target. |

Gaps no competitor fills for free:
1. A free 12-season quiz (most quizzes are 4-season, app-based or need a photo).
2. A result that shows the **closest alternative season** and says when the
   result is uncertain. SeasonApproved has a guide "can I be between two
   seasons", but no tool for it.
3. Copy and download of every palette as hex codes and PNG, without an
   account.
4. A comparison tool for **any two** seasons, not only a few fixed pairs.
5. One page per season that also explains the **alternative names**
   (Deep = Dark, True Summer = Cool Summer, and so on).

## 3. Keywords grouped by search intent

| Intent | What the searcher wants | Keyword groups | Page type |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Do (tool)** | Get an answer now | C, plus all of "what season am I" from B | Quiz page; the homepage hero sends people there |
| **Know (definition)** | Understand the system | A → homepage; D → 12 color seasons hub | Homepage, hub |
| **Know (how-to)** | Work it out themselves | "how to do color analysis at home" (B), G | Seasonal color analysis guide, Warm vs cool undertone |
| **Reference** | See colors and copy codes | E | 12 season palette pages. The hub contains the palette explorer. |
| **Compare (specific pair)** | Decide between two named seasons | F | One written page per commonly confused pair |
| **Compare (any pair)** | Put any two seasons side by side | – (no query of its own) | Comparison tool |
| **Apply** | Use the palette | H | Styling guides |
| **Trust** | Who is behind this | – | About, privacy policy |

### Duplicate-intent check (done)

Every keyword has exactly one target page. These overlaps were found and
removed:

| Overlap | Pages that competed | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| "12 season color palettes", "all color palettes" | `/12-color-seasons/` and `/color-palettes/` | **Merged.** The palette explorer is on the hub. `/color-palettes/` is no longer a page. The "Color Palettes" menu item goes to `/12-color-seasons/#palettes`. |
| "what season am I", "find my color season" | Quiz and seasonal color analysis guide | **Quiz only.** The guide targets the do-it-yourself queries ("how to do color analysis at home") and links to the quiz. |
| "what is seasonal color analysis" | Homepage and guide | **Homepage only.** The guide does not repeat the definition. It links to the homepage. |
| "{season} makeup", "best lipstick for {season}", "{season} clothes" | Season page and the makeup and wardrobe guides | **Season page.** The general guides target only the general queries (seasonal makeup colors, how to build a wardrobe from your palette). They link to the season pages and give no full per-season lists. |
| "dark autumn", "cool summer", other alternative names | Possible separate pages | **One page per season.** Alternative names are covered in the H1 summary and the FAQs. |
| "soft summer vs soft autumn" | Comparison tool and the written page | **Written page.** The tool page has no target query and links to the written comparisons. |
| "warm undertone colors" | Warm vs cool undertone and best colors for warm undertones | **Split by intent.** The first is "how to tell", the second is "what to wear". Neither repeats the other's main section. |

Rules that stop pages competing with each other:
- **One URL per intent**, as in the table above.
- **One page per season**, including all its alternative names. There are no
  separate "dark autumn" pages.
- The guide index `/color-guides/` is only for navigation. It has no target
  query.

Internal linking:
- Every season page links to its family siblings, its two closest
  neighbours (the seasons it is most often confused with), at least one
  comparison, the quiz and the hub.
- The quiz result links to the matched season page and the alternative.
- Every guide links to the quiz and to the relevant season pages.
- Breadcrumbs: Home › 12 Color Seasons › {Season}; Home › Color Guides ›
  {Guide}; Home › Compare Seasons › {Comparison}.

## 5. Page outlines

### Homepage, target "seasonal color analysis"
H1 Discover Your Perfect Colors with Seasonal Color Analysis → quiz preview
→ H2 The four color families and 12 seasons → H2 Popular color palettes →
H2 How seasonal color analysis works (undertone, depth, chroma, contrast)
→ H2 Commonly confused seasons → H2 Color guides → H2 FAQs → final CTA.

### Quiz, target "seasonal color analysis quiz"
H1 Free Seasonal Color Analysis Quiz → short intro (15 questions, about
3 minutes, no photo, no email) → quiz → H2 How the quiz works (four
dimensions, how the result and alternative are chosen) → H2 How accurate
is a quiz? (limits, daylight, draping) → H2 FAQs.

### Season page, target "{season} color palette"
H1 {Season} Color Palette → answer-first summary ("{Season} is a … season,
also called …") → palette with hex codes (copy, download) → H2 {Season}
characteristics (temperature, depth, chroma, contrast) → H2 Best clothing
colors → H2 Best neutrals → H2 Colors to wear with care → H2 Makeup → H2
Hair color → H2 Jewelry and metals → H2 Outfit combinations → H2 Is it
{Season} or {neighbour}? → H2 FAQs → H2 Related seasons.

### Comparison page, target "{a} vs {b}"
H1 {A} vs {B}: How to Tell the Difference → one-sentence answer → side-by-
side table (four dimensions) → palettes side by side → H2 What they share →
H2 The key difference → H2 Tests to try at home → H2 If you are still
unsure → FAQs.

### 12 Color Seasons hub, target "12 color seasons"
H1 The 12 Color Seasons → answer-first definition → table of the 12 (family,
temperature, depth, chroma, contrast) → H2 The four families → H2 All 12
palettes (palette explorer: copy, download) → H2 How the seasons connect
(neighbours) → FAQs.

### Guide page, target "how to do color analysis at home" / "warm vs cool undertone"
Answer first → numbered method → H2 per test → common mistakes → link to
the quiz → FAQs.

## 6. Structured data plan

- All pages: Organization, WebSite, WebPage, BreadcrumbList.
- Quiz: WebApplication (free offer). No ratings, because there are no real
  reviews.
- Season pages: Article, with `about` set to a DefinedTerm in a
  DefinedTermSet named "12 color seasons". Palettes as an ItemList.
- Comparisons and guides: Article.
- Homepage and guides: FAQPage only where the FAQ is visible on the page.

The PRD names one SEO plugin (Rank Math or Yoast) for titles, meta,
canonical tags and the sitemap. The plugin adds the season-specific schema.
Do not fake reviews, ratings or expert credentials.
